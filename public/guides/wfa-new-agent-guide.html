A note from our family to yours

We are so glad you are here.

Choosing to build a career in life insurance, especially with Watts Family Agency, is one of the boldest, most meaningful decisions you will ever make. It takes courage to bet on yourself. It takes vision to see what is possible. And it takes the right team around you to help you get there.

That is exactly what we are here to be.

Work through every page. Do the checklist. Track your dials. Read the books. Show up to the calls. And, most importantly, stay close to the fire. The people around you are your greatest asset.

We are proud of you for saying yes. Now let's go build something beautiful.

Do this now

Step 3AML certificate & E&O insurance

Get through this as close to immediately as you can — carrier contracting can take 2–10 business days to return a writing number. Several carriers let you write under a guest code in the meantime.

AML$15, about 10–15 minutes, via WebCE.E&OYou need a $1 million policy.
  • Besides the carrier linked in Fast Start, we also recommend Next Insurance. CalSurance is the more straightforward application; Next offers a pay-monthly option.
  • You don't need general liability or annuities (unless you already have annuity experience and plan to write them right away).
  • The effective date must be today — not a future date.
  • Your name must be the named insured. Not Symmetry, not your agency, not any other business name.
  • E&O from another industry (e.g. real estate) won't cover you here.

Save both certificates — you'll need them in the next step.

Step 4SureLC profile setup

Pause and read the requirements for the EFT document — this is the bank account your commissions are deposited into. If in doubt, call your bank for their direct deposit form; you don't need to order checks.

Getting the bank documents and signature right is critical to getting contracts approved the first time. Follow the instructions exactly.

As soon as you finish Step 4, text Andrew: 919-599-1639

Step 5Carrier contracting

After we review your SureLC profile, we'll create your carrier contracts. Each arrives as its own email — open each one, fill out the request, and submit.

!
Exception

United Home Life's request comes from producerexpress@sircon.com, not SureLC. Check Spam/Junk and Promotions. It usually arrives within an hour of your Welcome to Symmetry email — if you don't see it, let us know.

Contracts take 2–30 days depending on the carrier. As they come in, log in to each back office, bookmark it, and save your login. You don't need every carrier to start dialing — 5 or 6 is usually enough.

The triple dial

Triple dial each lead a minimum of 3 separate times in the first week.

3 rings, hang up→3 rings, hang up→3 rings, voicemail or text→Call again later

Culture & expectations

How we work, what we expect, and what winning agents do every week.

Stay close to the fire

Your environment shapes your outcomes

The fire is the energy, the momentum, the community, and the culture of this agency. Stay close to it and it changes everything. Distance yourself and the cold creeps in fast. Stay close. Stay burning.

01

Show up to the calls

The weekly calls are your lifeline. Consistent attendance is non-negotiable for agents who want to grow.

02

Stay connected to your mentor

You weren't built to do this alone. Check in weekly, ask questions, be coachable — the agents who win fastest stay humble and stay connected.

03

Protect your energy

What you consume mentally, physically, and spiritually matters. Read. Move your body. Guard your mindset. This business will test you.

04

Celebrate every win

First dial, first appointment, first policy, first promotion — none of it is small. Share your wins; that energy is contagious.

#DoBoth
You don't have to choose between a thriving business and a full life. Build the income. Be present for your family. This business is you versus you.

Massive action, not motion

Jump in, do it messy, do it scared. We can only mentor movement.

Don't be on calls just to be on calls. Don't train just to train. It's our job to help you clean up the mess and learn as you go.

The activity target

Present to ten families a week.

In the beginning this is your primary activity metric, full stop. "Dial until" is the operative phrase — there's no magic number, you keep dialing until you hit it. If a client no-shows, use that time to pick up the phone and replace the appointment.

Get a running buddy

Find a friend — or make one here — at the same stage as you to get licensed and run alongside. Not someone you're pulling along: someone to keep each other accountable.

Part six

Growth path

Everything up to now has been your foundation. From here on out, it's refinement of skills you already have — not new fundamentals.

Conferences

Be in the room

Our two semi-annual conferences are where the wider Symmetry community, top producers, and leadership all show up in one place — and they tend to be a real turning point for the agents who go. WFA agents can usually get a discount, but that's not the point. The point is being in the room.

Every springEngage
Every fallIgnite
Team building

The best time to start building a team was yesterday. The second best time is today.

It doesn't matter that you're new — you already know more than someone you'll bring in, and we're here to help mentor them and mentor you in your mentorship. This job is always better with a friend doing it beside you. Building isn't for everyone, but if you want a team someday, it's never too early to start.

A final note

Trust the process, the system, and your mentor.

We know this is a lot of new information and new skills. We're here to help every step of the way — and the best agents are the ones texting us and checking in regularly. Get in massive action and seek constant correction.

The only agent that fails is the one who quits while still in the learning curve. Take it one day and one dial at a time. Lean in to the Huddles, the MACC Room, and Slack. The next six weeks are your true cost of education. Get curious, not critical — and get excited to build your skills and protect a lot of families.
